WHO
WE ARE?

EFEX216 Architecture is a full-service architectural office that designs its projects with a team of expert architects and manages the entire process from design to implementation from a single center.

Each project is planned and executed with a single project management approach, encompassing design, production, logistics, and field implementation processes. This approach minimizes risks during implementation, strengthens coordination, and ensures projects are completed according to the planned schedule.

Our clients work not just with a stand supplier, but with a professional team that takes ownership of the project, manages the process transparently, and is accessible at every stage.

“10+ years of industry experience | Experience with 450+ completed projects”

With the experience we have gained both domestically and internationally, we consider fulfilling our commitments, maintaining our quality standards, and establishing long-term business partnerships as our most important responsibilities.
