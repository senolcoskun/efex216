2. Personal data we process

  • Identity data: your first and last name.
  • Contact data: your email address; your phone number if you contact us by phone or WhatsApp.
  • Request data: the subject and content of your message and the page you sent the form from.
  • Security data: IP address, date-time and browser information recorded in our service providers’ server logs when the form is submitted.

Please do not include sensitive personal data, such as health information, in your messages.

3. Purposes of processing

  • Responding to your contact requests, questions and requests for proposals,
  • Preparing proposals for the project you request and conducting pre-contract discussions,
  • Keeping records of communications and using them as evidence in possible disputes,
  • Keeping the website secure and preventing misuse (spam),
  • Fulfilling our legal obligations.

4. Collection method and legal grounds

Your personal data is collected through the contact form on our website, email, phone and WhatsApp, partly by automated means, and is processed on the following legal grounds under Article 5(2) of KVKK:

  • (c) It is directly related to the conclusion or performance of a contract (requests for proposals),
  • (ç) It is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation of the data controller,
  • (e) It is necessary for the establishment, exercise or protection of a right,
  • (f) It is necessary for our legitimate interests, provided it does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms (general enquiries and website security).

5. Transfers of personal data

Your personal data may be transferred, limited to the purposes above, to:

  • Our service providers: the form service that forwards contact form messages to us by email (Web3Forms), and our email and website hosting (GitHub Pages) providers,
  • Competent public authorities: upon request, within the scope of our legal obligations.

As the servers of our form, email and hosting providers are located outside Türkiye, data you send through the contact form is transferred abroad. Such transfers are carried out in accordance with the conditions set out in Article 9 of KVKK. If you do not want your data transferred abroad, you can reach us by phone.

6. Third-party content and cookies

Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts and the map on the contact page from Google Maps. These services may process technical information such as your IP address and use their own cookies, subject to Google’s privacy policy. If you use the WhatsApp button, your conversation takes place on the WhatsApp (Meta) platform.

Our website uses your browser’s local storage to remember your theme preference (light/dark) and whether the intro animation has been shown. This information contains no personal data and is not sent to our servers. We do not use advertising or visitor-tracking cookies.

7. Retention

Your personal data is kept for as long as the purpose of processing requires and until the retention periods set by applicable law expire; it is then deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

8. Your rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK you have the right to:

  • Learn whether your personal data is processed,
  • Request information if it has been processed,
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used in line with that purpose,
  • Know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred,
  • Request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate,
  • Request deletion or destruction under the conditions of Article 7 of KVKK,
  • Request that third parties to whom it was transferred be notified of correction, deletion or destruction,
  • Object to a result against you arising exclusively from automated analysis,
  • Claim compensation if you suffer damage due to unlawful processing.

9. How to apply

You can submit requests regarding your rights, in line with the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller:

  • In writing (with a wet-ink signature) to our address above, together with documents verifying your identity, or
  • Via registered electronic mail (KEP), a secure electronic or mobile signature, or from an email address you have previously given us and that is registered in our systems, to info@efex216.com.

Your request will be concluded free of charge as soon as possible and within thirty days at the latest, depending on its nature. If the process requires an additional cost, a fee may be charged according to the tariff set by the Personal Data Protection Board.
